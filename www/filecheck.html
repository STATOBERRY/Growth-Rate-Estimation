Uploaded file Health Check Instructions

Inspect datatable shown below for the following:

  1. Check the datatable below:
    • The year column should appear in green (numbers such as 2001, 2002, ...). ✔
    • Every series you want to analyse (for example Area, Production, Yield) should appear in green. ✔
    • A district or region column, if present, is text and appears in yellow. Text group labels are valid. ✔
    • Colours help check data types; they do not prove the series are correct. Each year should appear once, unless the file has one row per district or region and a Group column is chosen.
  2. Identify and Correct Errors:
    • If a year or series column appears in yellow, its numbers are being read as text. Inspect it for issues such as:
      • Spaces between numbers or thousands separators (1,820)
      • Incorrect decimal points
      • Text entries such as "NA", "-" or "n/a"
    • Carefully review these yellow-highlighted columns and correct them in your original file. ✘
  3. Address Missing Values:
    • Cells highlighted in red indicate missing values. ✘
    • A missing year is left out of the calculations for that series only; it is not replaced or filled in.
    • Leave a genuinely missing year blank. Do not type 0: a zero is treated as a real value, and compound growth rates (CGR, CAGR) cannot use zero or negative values.
    • Some rows or columns may appear red even if they seem empty. This usually happens if there are residual values after deletion. Select the affected rows or columns in your Excel file, right-click, and choose "Clear Contents", or delete them entirely.
