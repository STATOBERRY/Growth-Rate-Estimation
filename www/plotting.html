Mastering Your Plots: Tips to Perfect Every Detail

Welcome to Plots & Graphs in RAISINS! Here, we provide stunning, publication-ready plots for your data. Since plot quality depends on the nature of your data, some plots might look fantastic, while others need a bit of tweaking. But don’t worry! Simply click the gear icon at the top left of any plot to access Plot Settings – where you can adjust titles, colors, themes, and more to suit your needs.

Let’s go over a few quick tips to keep your plots looking top-notch and troubleshoot any common issues!

Customizing the Plot – You’re in Charge!

Each plot has a Settings gear icon in the top left corner, giving you full control. Use it to edit titles, colors, themes—everything you need to get your plots just right. Adjust away and make each plot your own!

Download in High Quality

Need a polished image? Just click the Download Plot button at the bottom, and you’ll get a clean 300 dpi PNG, perfect for presentations and reports.

When the Number of Characters and Treatments is High

Plots tend to look cleaner when the number of treatments and characters isn’t too high. So, if you have many characters, consider heading to the Analysis.Results tab to select only the ones you need, then return to Plots & Graphs—no need to hit the Run Analysis button again. After just one character? Simply select that character for a streamlined view!

Fixing Overlapping Labels and Small Plot Size

  • When Labels Overlap: Lots of treatments can lead to crowded labels. Go to Settings and adjust the x-axis label size to keep it readable.
  • When Plots Shrink: More than 10 characters? Select Divide the Plot (Columns) in Settings and add columns until your plot has the space it needs.

Go Light for Better Clarity

If the plot still feels too busy, go back to Analysis.Results and limit the characters—fewer characters make for a cleaner, more readable plot.

Explore, Experiment & Enjoy!

With so many customization options, your plots can be as unique as your data. Experiment with themes and colors and enjoy bringing your analysis to life in RAISINS!
