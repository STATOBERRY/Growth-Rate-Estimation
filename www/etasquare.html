Generalized Eta Squared in ANOVA

Generalized Eta Squared is an effect size measure used in analysis of variance (ANOVA), particularly suitable for repeated measures and mixed designs.

Calculation of Generalized Eta Squared

Generalized Eta Squared is calculated by dividing the sum of squares for the effect of interest by the total variance, including variability due to subjects (for repeated measures). The formula is:

η²G = SSeffect / (SSeffect + SSerror + SSsubjects)

Where:

  • SSeffect = Sum of squares for the factor (e.g., Treatment)
  • SSerror = Sum of squares for error/residual
  • SSsubjects = Sum of squares for subjects (within-subject variability)

Importance of Generalized Eta Squared

Generalized Eta Squared is valuable for several reasons:

  • Unbiased in Repeated Measures: It properly accounts for within-subject variability, unlike traditional Eta Squared.
  • Comparability: Results are comparable across studies, even with different designs (e.g., repeated vs. between-subjects).
  • Effect Size Clarity: It quantifies how much variance is explained, making interpretation of ANOVA more meaningful.
  • Use in Software: It is supported in R using packages like afex and effectsize.

Interpretation Benchmarks

As a guideline, Cohen's suggested benchmarks for interpreting η²G are:

Effect Size η²G Value
Small ≈ 0.01
Medium ≈ 0.06
Large ≈ 0.14
