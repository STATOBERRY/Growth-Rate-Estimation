Perform Repeated Measures ANOVA in RAISINS

1 Upload Your Excel or CSV File
Upload your dataset in .xls, .xlsx, or .csv format. CSV format is recommended for faster processing.
2 Verify Uploaded Columns
Once the dataset is uploaded, RAISINS automatically detects the number of rows and columns in your file. Review the detected column names carefully. If everything looks correct, click "Got it, let's go!" to proceed to the analysis setup.
3 Select Treatment, time, and Study Variable Columns
Choose the appropriate Treatment column, time/day column, and the character(s) under study.
4 Select Transformation (If Needed)
If your data violates assumptions, you may select appropriate transformation options before running the analysis.
5 Click Run Analysis
Click the Run Analysis button to generate statistical results.
Go through all the tabs to access complete tables, plots, and AI-based interpretation.
