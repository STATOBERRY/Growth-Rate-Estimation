How to prepare and upload your file?

The module works on yearly time-series data: one row per year (or time point) and one column for every series you want to study, such as Area, Production or Yield. Save the sheet as CSV or Excel (.xlsx / .xls) and upload it from the sidebar of the Data & Settings tab.

  • Year column: numbers only (2001, 2002, …). Choose this column in the Year column picker. Agricultural years written as 2001-02 should be entered as a single year such as 2001.
  • Series columns: every numeric column other than the year is offered in the Series columns picker. Select the ones you want to analyse.
  • At least 3 years of data are needed; 10 or more give far more reliable trends.

Panel data (several regions or districts)

If the file has a text column such as District or Region, with one row per year and district, choose it in the Group column picker. Additive series such as area and production are summed by year across a fixed set of districts. A missing component value makes the annual total unavailable. Supply yield and other ratios as correctly weighted annual series.

Good practice

  • Keep the first row as column names and avoid merged cells, notes or totals below the data.
  • Use plain names such as Area and Production; the decomposition step looks for these words in the column names.
  • Keep units consistent across years (for example, always hectares and tonnes).
  • Leave a missing year blank rather than typing 0; a zero is treated as a real value.

Running the analysis

After the upload, check the three pickers, tick the analyses you need under Trend analyses to run and press Run Analysis!. The level of significance, font and table style can then be changed in the green options panel above the results. In Preview Mode, uploads are disabled: choose one of the built-in coconut datasets instead.
